Focus Player

Settings. Everything here stays on this computer.

Blocks well-known ad and pop-up networks everywhere. While Focus Player is active on a tab, it also closes pop-up tabs that tab tries to open (real links you click still open).

While focused, mutes every other sound on the page: chat beeps, ad audio, notification pings. The video you're watching keeps its sound. With Deep mode this also covers chat boxes and widgets loaded from other sites.

If the picture stops for about 15 seconds while locked, it jumps back to live, then restarts the player.

Lets Focus Player work inside embedded players (most sites use them): working Play/Volume buttons, exact freeze detection, staying live, and blocking pop-ups and sounds inside frames. Chrome asks for access to all sites. It only does anything in a tab where Focus Player is on.
