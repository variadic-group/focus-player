Focus Player

Theater mode for every website. Settings stay on this computer.

Blocks well-known ad and pop-up networks everywhere. In theater mode, the page can't open pop-up windows or tabs.

In theater mode, everything else on the page is muted: autoplay ads, chat pings, notification sounds. Your video keeps its sound.

If a playing stream gets stuck, it catches up to live, then restarts the player. A paused video is always left alone.

Most sites play video inside a player from another site. This lets speed, pop-out, Go live, stall recovery and muting work inside those players too. Chrome asks for access to all sites; it's only used when you use Focus Player on a tab.
